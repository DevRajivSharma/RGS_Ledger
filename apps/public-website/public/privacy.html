Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Platform: RGS Ledger (https://rgsledger.rajivsharma.online) • Operator: Rajiv Sharma

1. Introduction & Overview

RGS Ledger (“we”, “our”, or “the platform”), operated by Rajiv Sharma and accessible at https://rgsledger.rajivsharma.online, provides a specialized non-custodial software solution for Unified Payments Interface (UPI) payment collection and automated dual-source credit-alert evidence matching.

This Privacy Policy discloses how we collect, process, store, encrypt, and delete information when merchants register, configure bank accounts, connect Google accounts for Gmail credit notification synchronization, or utilize our Android Companion application. We are committed to absolute transparency, data minimization, and the highest standards of financial data protection.

2. Core FinTech Architecture & Zero-Custody Disclosures

Strict Non-Custodial Boundary: RGS Ledger does NOT hold merchant or customer funds, does NOT operate customer or merchant wallets, does NOT issue or manage debit/credit cards, does NOT connect to direct bank core APIs, and does NOT execute inter-bank fund transfers or settlement.

When customers pay via UPI QR codes generated by RGS Ledger, payments move directly from the customer’s bank account across the NPCI UPI network to the merchant’s own configured bank account.

Payment Status vs. Bank Confirmation: A payment status of SUCCESS in RGS Ledger signifies solely that our dual-source evidence matching policy was satisfied (i.e. both an authorized bank credit email and an Android companion push notification matched the same transaction amount and UTR). It does NOT constitute a direct, bank-authoritative confirmation from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), NPCI, or underlying banking switches.

3. Google API Services & Gmail OAuth Integration

To automate the matching of bank credit alerts, merchants may optionally connect their Google Account to allow RGS Ledger to inspect incoming bank alert emails.

A. Scope Requested

RGS Ledger requests strictly one read-only Gmail scope:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

B. Purpose of Processing

The sole purpose of requesting this permission is to identify and parse incoming bank credit alert emails that confirm a customer deposit into the merchant’s bank account. This evidence is correlated against pending payment requests generated by the merchant.

C. What Email Data is Accessed and Extracted

  • Filtering by Sender: Our sync engine inspects only incoming emails sent by recognized financial institutions and bank sender domains (e.g. Union Bank of India, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, State Bank of India, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank) matching merchant-approved regex notification templates.
  • Data Extracted: From qualifying bank emails, the engine extracts strictly:
    • Transaction credit amount in paise (e.g. ₹10.00 = 1000 paise).
    • Bank Unique Transaction Reference (UTR / RRN / Ref Number).
    • Masked bank account suffix (e.g. *0562 or XX1234).
    • Transaction timestamp.
    • Credit status indicator (verifying credit rather than debit alerts).

D. What Email Data is NOT Accessed

  • We do NOT access, read, or process personal communications, correspondence with friends or family, business contracts, or non-banking emails.
  • We do NOT access email drafts, sent messages, trash, or outbox.
  • We do NOT download or store email file attachments.
  • We do NOT have permission to compose, send, modify, forward, or delete emails.

E. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

RGS Ledger’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • No AI Model Training: Google user data is NEVER used or transferred to develop, train, or fine-tune generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • No Advertising or Commercial Sale: Google user data is NEVER sold, rented, leased, or transferred to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms.
  • Human Access Controls: No human employees, contractors, or administrators read merchant email content unless:
    1. The merchant gives explicit, affirmative consent for specific technical troubleshooting of a failed template extraction;
    2. Required for security investigation (such as investigating malicious abuse); or
    3. Required to comply with applicable law or legal process.

4. Android Companion Push Notification Evidence Collection

The optional RGS Ledger Android Companion application installed on merchant Android devices or counter terminals provides the second independent leg of evidence verification:

  • Native Permission: Utilizes Android’s native NotificationListenerService (guarded by the system permission BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE). This requires explicit, manual authorization by the merchant in Android Settings.
  • Restricted Package Scope: The companion app filters incoming notifications on-device, capturing notifications strictly originating from merchant-enabled banking packages and SMS applications containing credit alert keywords.
  • No Personal Data Access: The companion cannot access contact books, phone call logs, photos, media files, or personal messaging applications (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, or personal chat apps).
  • On-Device Encryption & WorkManager Sync: Captured notifications are saved in an encrypted local SQLite database on the Android terminal and synchronized to the RGS Ledger API using Android WorkManager. Notifications on the device are automatically pruned after 7 days.

5. OAuth Token Storage, Encryption & Security Architecture

RGS Ledger enforces defense-in-depth cryptographic protections for all stored credentials:

  • AES-256-GCM Encryption at Rest: All Google OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with unique initialization vectors (IVs) and authentication tags.
  • Cryptographic Payload Hashing: Raw notification payloads are encrypted at rest and indexed using SHA-256 digests (rawPayloadHash) to prevent duplicate processing.
  • Strict Tenant Partitioning: Every database read, write, or lookup query is strictly partitioned by merchant tenant ID (merchantId). No merchant can view another merchant’s evidence or configurations.
  • Short-Lived JWT Sessions: Merchant dashboard access uses short-lived JWT access tokens (15 minutes) paired with cryptographically signed, secure refresh tokens.

6. Data Retention, Account Disconnection & Deletion

  • Evidence Retention Window: Extracted transaction evidence (amounts, UTRs, timestamps) is retained for 90 days for operational payment reconciliation and merchant tax reporting (configurable up to 365 days by platform administration).
  • Disconnecting Gmail Integration:

    Merchants may revoke RGS Ledger’s access to Gmail at any time through either of two methods:

    1. In the RGS Ledger Merchant Dashboard: Navigate to Integrations → Gmail → click Disconnect Integration.
    2. In your Google Account: Navigate to Google Account Security → Third-party apps with account access → select RGS Ledger → click Remove Access.

    Upon disconnection, stored OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are immediately and permanently deleted from our database.

  • Permanent Account Deletion: Merchants can request the complete erasure of their account, business profile, API keys, device pairings, and evidence records by emailing privacy@rajivsharma.online. Data erasure requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

7. Third-Party Infrastructure & Data Sharing

RGS Ledger operates on reputable cloud infrastructure providers bound by enterprise data processing agreements:

  • Database: MongoDB Atlas (AES-256 encrypted database clusters).
  • API Backend: Render (isolated containerized service).
  • Frontend Hosting & CDN: Cloudflare Pages / Vercel (static content delivery).

We do NOT share, sell, rent, or transfer merchant or customer data to any other third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

8. User Rights

Under applicable data privacy laws, merchants have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal and business data we hold about them.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate profile or bank account details.
  • Revoke OAuth authorizations at any time.
  • Request the permanent deletion of their account and historical transaction records.
