3. Dual-Source Evidence Policy & Payment Status Meanings

Payment status updates in RGS Ledger reflect the satisfaction of our internal dual-source matching rules:

  • PENDING: The payment request QR has been generated; awaiting customer payment and incoming credit alerts.
  • PARTIAL_SUCCESS: Exactly ONE evidence source (Email OR Android notification) matched the payment request.
  • SUCCESS: BOTH merchant-authorized Bank Email AND Android Companion evidence matched the same transaction amount and UTR reference.
  • REVIEW_REQUIRED: Conflicting or ambiguous evidence was detected (e.g. amount mismatch or duplicate UTR).
  • EXPIRED: Payment request window passed without receiving sufficient matching evidence.

FinTech Clarification: A payment status of SUCCESS signifies that our dual-source evidence criteria were satisfied. It does NOT constitute a direct bank-authoritative confirmation from the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) or the underlying banking rails.
